### Changes

- **Cue runners start sooner.** A cue now times each runner by the 95th percentile of its label's recent boots, not the 75th, and starts it 5 s earlier still. Before, in most 8-job pushes at least one cued runner came up after its job had queued. Each cued runner idles a little longer before its job, about the gap between those two percentiles plus 5 s.
