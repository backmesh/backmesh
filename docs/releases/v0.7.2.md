Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **Spot labels fall back to on-demand on their own.** When spot capacity is refused in every runner region, jobs start on demand (billed at on-demand rates) and return to spot after 30 minutes. ([#159](https://github.com/backmesh/backmesh/pull/159))
- **Runners in the EU.** `backmesh install --runner-geo eu`, or `backmesh upgrade --runner-geo eu`, runs an org's runners and their cache in EU member states' regions only, such as GCP's `europe-west4`; the default stays `us`, and a re-run or an upgrade keeps the org's geo.
- **High-memory labels.** `backmesh-ubuntu-2404-highmem-2vcpu` to `-16vcpu`, and their on-demand twins `backmesh-ubuntu-2404-highmem-od-<n>vcpu`, have 8 GiB a vCPU: AWS `r8a` to `r6a`, GCP `n2-highmem` and `n2d-highmem`, Azure `E<n>ads_v5` to `E<n>s_v5`. `backmesh upgrade` adds them.
