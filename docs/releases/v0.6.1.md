Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **AWS on-demand labels try `m8a` first.** `backmesh-od-2` to `backmesh-od-16` and their `backmesh-aws-od-<n>` twins now try AMD's `m8a`, then `m8i`, `m7a`, `m7i` and `m6a` as before: at the same vCPUs `m8a` ran backmesh's builds in 0.64–0.88 times `m8i`'s time, typically about 1.3 times as fast, about 9 % cheaper a job at `backmesh-od-4` and about even at `backmesh-od-16`. It counts against the same "Running On-Demand Standard" quota (`L-1216C47A`). Spot, KVM and GPU labels are unchanged: `m8a`'s spot capacity is too scarce, and it doesn't nest. `backmesh upgrade` lists the on-demand labels under "New VM types or regions" and asks before it applies them.
