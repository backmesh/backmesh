Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **AWS labels try `m8a` first.** `backmesh-2` to `backmesh-16`, their on-demand and `backmesh-public-<n>` labels, and their `backmesh-aws-<n>` twins now try AMD's `m8a`, then `m8i`, `m7a`, `m7i` and `m6a` as before. At the same vCPUs `m8a` ran backmesh's builds in 0.64–0.88 times `m8i`'s time, typically about 1.3 times as fast. A zone with no spot `m8a` now costs a job seconds, not the 25–55 s it took before: anycloud moves on to the next zone or type as soon as one refuses. In a test of 20 launches each, spot `m8a.xlarge` landed 20 times in about 10 s, and spot `m8a.4xlarge` 9 times in about 14 s. On spot, a `backmesh-4` job costs less, since `m8a.xlarge` costs about the same an hour as `m8i.xlarge`, and a `backmesh-16` job about the same; on demand a job costs about 9 % less at `backmesh-od-4` and about the same at `backmesh-od-16`. `m8a` counts against the same Standard spot and on-demand quotas (`L-34B43A08` and `L-1216C47A`). KVM and GPU labels are unchanged: `m8a` doesn't nest. `backmesh upgrade` lists the labels under "New VM types or regions" and asks before it applies them.
- **The cloud CLIs install without apt.** On first use, `az`, `gcloud` and `aws` now install from pinned vendor releases: Azure CLI 2.93.0, Google Cloud CLI 593.0.0 and AWS CLI 2.40.4, the versions GitHub's Ubuntu 24.04 image has. Each release is checked against its SHA-256 or AWS's signature, and failed downloads are retried. apt package indexes are no longer fetched, so a mirror mid-sync or returning a 5xx no longer fails the step. `gsutil` now installs on first use too.
- **Cue runners start sooner.** A cue now times each runner by the 95th percentile of its label's recent boots, not the 75th, and starts it 5 s earlier still. Before, in most 8-job pushes at least one cued runner came up after its job had queued. Each cued runner idles a little longer before its job, about the gap between those two percentiles plus 5 s.
