Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **The automatic caches hold a job up for at most 10 seconds.** In v0.5.3, Set up runner waited up to 30 seconds on every job, hit or miss, and most of that wait was for the job's cache token, not the restores. The listener saves the token when the job starts, and the token reaches the runner when anycloud next updates the runner's Secret, up to 30 seconds later. A job now waits for it at most 10 seconds, then starts without its automatic caches.
- **Set up runner says where the time went.** A line such as `backmesh cache: auto took 9412 ms: 5180 ms for the cache token, 4232 ms restoring` follows the restores. A job that restores nothing says why: no token in time, no record of the job yet, the job opted out, a `container:` job, or the cache off for the runner. Before, these jobs waited and printed nothing.
- **A tool the job changed after setup no longer misses on every run.** A Python that `pip install` wrote into isn't saved, but its job's record still listed it, so each later run printed `auto missed Python <version>`. The record now lists only the tools that were saved.

### Known limitations

- **The cache token can come too late for a job's restores.** Until anycloud delivers a saved Secret to its running Jobs at once, a token that takes longer than 10 seconds leaves the job without its automatic caches, and Set up runner prints `no cache token within 10 s`. `backmesh/cache` steps wait for the token on their own, up to 20 seconds.
