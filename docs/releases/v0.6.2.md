Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **GCP's spot labels try AMD's `c4d` first.** `backmesh-4`, `backmesh-8` and `backmesh-16` on GCP, and `backmesh-public-4`, now try `c4d` before `n2` and `n2d`; `backmesh-2` and KVM labels don't change. Spot `c4d-standard-4` launched 13 times of 13 in `us-east4` with none reclaimed ([anycloud#38](https://github.com/anycloud-sh/anycloud/pull/38)). Its disk is Hyperdisk Balanced at 3,000 IOPS and 750 MiB/s, not on demand's 8,000 and 1,000: in an A/B on `c4d-standard-4`, a job's VM time was 1.02 of on demand's disk's, its image pulls 1.04, its `docker build` 1.03 and its kernel-to-container start 1.06 (p = 0.050) ([anycloud#43](https://github.com/anycloud-sh/anycloud/pull/43)). A spot job would cost about 1.05 of one on `n2` for about 0.6 of its VM time, estimated at `c4d-standard-4`'s catalog spot price ($0.0967 an hour in `us-east4`, the VM alone) against `n2`'s VM time and cost per job from on-demand runs in `us-central1` ([anycloud#38](https://github.com/anycloud-sh/anycloud/pull/38), [anycloud#43](https://github.com/anycloud-sh/anycloud/pull/43)). Spot runners now count against `C4D_CPUS` where `PREEMPTIBLE_CPUS` isn't set, and a `c4d` runner's disk against the region's Hyperdisk Balanced quotas. `backmesh upgrade` lists the labels under "New VM types or regions" and asks before it applies them. ([#129](https://github.com/backmesh/backmesh/pull/129))
