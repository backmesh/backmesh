Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall.

### Changes

- **A smaller control plane VM, $24–$43 a month less.** A new installation's control plane runs on 2 vCPUs and 4 GiB: `t3.medium` on AWS, `e2-medium` on GCP, and on Azure `Standard_B2als_v2`, then `Standard_B2s`, then the 8 GiB sizes from before, whichever the region offers your subscription. One org's always-on VMs now cost about $56 a month on AWS, $50 on GCP and $68 on Azure, instead of $86, $74 and $111. An existing control plane keeps its VM. ([#PR](https://github.com/backmesh/backmesh/pull/PR))
