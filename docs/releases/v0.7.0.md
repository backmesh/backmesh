Upgrade with `backmesh upgrade`; there's no need to uninstall. `upgrade` lists each label it renames, old to new, and asks first. Change your workflows' `runs-on` right after: a job sent to an old label waits until GitHub cancels it after 24 hours.

### Changes

- **Labels name the OS and vCPUs (breaking).** `backmesh-4` is now `backmesh-ubuntu-2404-4vcpu`; a cloud, `kvm-`, `public-` and `od-` keep their places (`backmesh-gcp-ubuntu-2404-kvm-od-8vcpu`), and `backmesh-gpu-t4` is `backmesh-ubuntu-2404-gpu-t4`. Old labels stop matching. ([#142](https://github.com/backmesh/backmesh/pull/142))
