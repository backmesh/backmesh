`hyperframes/` still holds the 22.0 s cut. The maintainers asked for this redo from
the page merged in anycloud-sh/backmesh#281 (2c631d6, live at https://backmesh.com), in its order:
the hero cost claim, credits, the agent-framed speed section, one VM per job,

the real job-log capture, and the cue footnote "median, on AWS".

transitions, the beat glow and the brand stay as they are.
